Privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026 · English translation of the Spanish original, which prevails.

In short

This website uses no cookies and no forms, and its fonts and videos are served from the website itself. It counts visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and does not store your IP address: only aggregate figures. The only other external element is the Payhip purchase button, which opens the checkout. We only process your data if you buy THE CARTRIDGE, and only to deliver it to you and to meet our obligations to the Spanish tax authorities.

Data controller

The owner of pixelogic0, whose details appear in the legal notice. Email: pixelogic0.ayuda@proton.me.

What data, and what for

Visiting the websiteThe service that hosts the website (GitHub Pages) records technical data, such as your IP address, in order to serve and protect it. We do not see or use that data.
Counting visitsWe use GoatCounter (the count.js code is loaded from gc.zgo.at) to know how many people visit the website and where they come from. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and does not store your IP address. It uses your IP address and browser, in memory only and for at most 8 hours, to tell unique visits apart and to work out your country. It keeps only aggregate figures: page viewed, where you came from, browser and system, screen width, country and language. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing whether the website works. If you don’t want to be counted, a tracker blocker usually prevents it, and the website works just the same.
Purchase buttonThe main page loads the Payhip button code (payhip.js) from Payhip’s servers, so Payhip receives technical data about your visit, such as your IP address. What Payhip does with it is governed by its privacy policy.
BuyingPayment is made on Payhip and its payment gateway (Stripe or PayPal). We receive your name, your email, your country and the order details; never your card details. We use them to deliver your files, provide support, issue the invoice and comply with VAT rules. Legal basis: the purchase contract and our tax obligations.
UpdatesOnly if you tick the updates box at checkout will we email you when there is a new version of the manual. You can unsubscribe at any time. Legal basis: your consent.
Writing to usIf you write to us, we use your email address and what you tell us only to reply to you.

How long

Purchase data, for as long as tax and commercial laws require. Support emails, for as long as your query lasts. The updates list, until you unsubscribe.

Who it is shared with

Only with the services that make the purchase and the website possible: Payhip, the payment gateway you use (Stripe or PayPal), the website host and GoatCounter (which stores its data in Finland and Germany). They may process data outside the European Economic Area, with the safeguards explained in their own privacy policies. We do not sell or disclose your data to anyone else.

Your rights

You can request access to, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability of your data, object to its processing, and withdraw your consent, by writing to pixelogic0.ayuda@proton.me. If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, aepd.es).

Language and governing law
